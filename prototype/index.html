HabitCrafts · redesign prototype · v1

A quiet paper studio where everything you touch springs back, and the things you keep turn gold.

Paper and springs. Everything you look at is paper — warm parchment canvas, matte white cards with hairline borders, blue as ink, one deep-blue hero surface per screen, grain instead of gloss. Everything you touch is a spring — it compresses under your finger, resists, and snaps back with a little overshoot. Calm is a material quality; fun is a behavioural one. They stop fighting because they live in different channels.

Type

Fraunces over Figtree — a soft variable serif against a friendly geometric sans. Fraunces is a daily voice, not an event voice: it takes h1 page titles, streak numerals, milestone headlines, empty-state headings and the Create Habit step questions. Body, labels, buttons and nav stay strictly Figtree. Minimum text size is 13px — the app's 10px card lines are abolished. No webfont is loaded anywhere in this prototype; the stacks degrade to the best local humanist sans and transitional serif.

Motion

Four curves and three spring approximations. CSS has no true spring, so the spring tokens are Google's own published beziers for the Material 3 Expressive spatial springs, each paired with its intended duration. A spatial spring never touches a colour or opacity transition — the overshoot produces a visible tint wobble, which is why Material's effects springs are critically damped. Press a track to run it.

ease-standard · 260ms

(0.2, 0, 0, 1) — the default

ease-enter · 260ms

(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1) — arriving

ease-exit · 200ms

(0.3, 0, 0.8, 0.15) — leaving

0.8/200 — sheets, milestone

0.8/380 — toggles, button release

0.6/800 — reward pop only

Gilding — the mark, forever

Day 7 leaves a 16px gold corner tick; day 30 adds a full inner hairline; day 100 adds an embossed seal beside the title; day 365 fills the seal solid. Cosmetic only, never lost, and it survives streak breaks — the gild records what you did, not what you are maintaining.

Empty state · calendar cells

One flat two-colour illustration and a single 4s drift — the only looping animation permitted in the product. Calendar cells carry three redundant states (fill, glyph and a per-cell label) so nothing is encoded by colour alone, and the encoding survives grayscale.

What this prototype refuses to do

  • No glassmorphism, no frosted cards, no glass modals.
  • No purple, no mesh or rainbow gradients, no animated gradient backgrounds.
  • No neumorphism, no inner-shadow pressed-plastic controls.
  • No drop shadows on resting content.
  • No confetti on ordinary completions; no celebration that loops or repeats in a session.
  • No scroll-triggered reveals, no parallax, no scroll-jacking.
  • No streak-guilt: no broken chains, no red missed days, no "don't lose your streak."
  • No AI-sparkle iconography, no glow on light surfaces, no emoji as interface elements.
  • No legacy-blue page background, and no raster chrome anywhere.
  • No new hex value outside the palette above. The palette is closed.

Zero external dependencies — no CDN, no webfont, no remote image, no build step. Hand-written CSS and vanilla JS; every file opens from file://. Architecture and class names: prototype/README-buildnotes.md.
